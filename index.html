How it works

Plug in, press Upload, watch it run.

No board menus to hunt through and no separate tools to open. The whole loop lives in one window, and every step has a shortcut.

  1. 1

    Pick and plug in

    Search 400+ boards by name, from Dev Modules to M5Stack, Adafruit and Seeed XIAO. S3 Forge spots your board on USB and picks the port.

    COM6 · Espressif USB
  2. 2

    Verify Ctrl+R

    Compile and see exactly how much flash and RAM your sketch uses. Errors jump straight to the line.

    Sketch uses 297282 bytes (22%)
  3. 3

    Upload Ctrl+U

    Compile, connect, write, verify and boot in one go. A board stuck in download mode gets rebooted into your code.

    Writing at 0x00010000 (64%)
  4. 4

    Watch Ctrl+M

    Connect to the board, then read the serial monitor or watch your values on the live plotter.

    temp:23.40 hum:48.10
Upload Ctrl+U

Uploads you can follow.

Every upload shows its five stages and a live percentage, so you always know whether it is compiling, talking to the chip or writing flash. Send it to the background and keep editing.

  • Compile, connect, write, verify and boot
  • Automatic reboot out of download mode
  • Serial monitor reconnects on its own after uploading

Firmware flasher

Flash MicroPython, CircuitPython or any .bin.

esptool is built in. Put a merged image at 0x0, or load the bootloader, partition table and app separately, then press Flash.

  • Flash at up to 921600 baud, with optional full erase
  • Read the chip's type, MAC, flash size and PSRAM
  • Reset or fully erase a board from the same panel

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Which boards does it support?

Any ESP32 board the Arduino IDE supports. S3 Forge reads the full board list from the ESP32 Arduino core, 400+ boards across the ESP32, S2, S3, C2, C3, C5, C6, H2 and P4, and shows each board's own options like flash size and partition scheme. If the connected chip doesn't match the board you picked, it tells you.

Do I need the Arduino IDE?

No. S3 Forge compiles with the free Arduino CLI, finds it automatically, and can install the ESP32 board package for you. Flashing ready-made .bin files needs nothing else.

Is the AI Coder free?

It uses your own API key. OpenRouter offers free models, so you can start without paying anything. Claude and GPT bill to your own account with those providers.

Where is my API key stored?

On your PC, in S3 Forge's settings. If you sign in to sync, it is also saved to your account so your other PCs have it. It is only ever sent to the AI provider you picked.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without signing in except the community gallery and syncing between PCs.

Does it run on Mac or Linux?

Not yet. S3 Forge is Windows-only for now.

What happens after I buy?

You get a download link for the Windows installer. Run it, open S3 Forge, and it starts with a blink sketch loaded and ready to upload.
